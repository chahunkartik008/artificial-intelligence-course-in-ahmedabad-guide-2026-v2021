What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence encompasses a range of technologies and methodologies designed to enable machines to mimic human cognitive functions. These functions include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding. By leveraging vast amounts of data and sophisticated algorithms, AI applications can analyze trends, make decisions, and automate processes, thereby improving efficiency and productivity across industries.
